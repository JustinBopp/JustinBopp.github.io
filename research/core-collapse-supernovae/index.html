Research Project

Disk Outflows

3D GRMHD collapsar simulations exploring how black hole spin, disk magnetic fields, and accretion physics shape jet launching and disk-driven outflows.

Back to Research

Project Team

  • Advisor: Dr. Ore Gottlieb, Center for Computational Astrophysics, Flatiron Institute, now at MIT

Project Overview

This project follows collapsar evolution in 3D GRMHD with H-AMR, using a suite of models that vary black hole spin while keeping the stellar envelope and magnetic setup fixed.

The main result is that large-scale, magnetically driven outflows can emerge even when the black hole spin is zero. In the low-spin runs, the disk itself powers subrelativistic outflows consistent with a Blandford-Payne-like picture, while higher-spin models develop more efficient and persistent jets.

That makes this project useful for bridging ordinary core-collapse explosions and fully jet-driven collapsars: spin strengthens the engine, but the disk and magnetic field can already produce meaningful outflows before Blandford-Znajek power dominates.
